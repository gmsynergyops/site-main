# Cancer Survivors — Banner Visual & Creative Direction Brief

> **Scope:** All 3 Survivors Section Pages (`/survivors/[slug]`)  
> **Component Target:** `components/Modules/survivors/SurvivorPage.tsx`  
> **Container Specs:** `h-100 md:h-125` (400px - 500px height, ~1280px max-width, ~2.5:1 ultra-wide aspect ratio) with bottom-overlay hero typography.  
> **Brand Palette:**  
> - **Primary Dominant:** Synergy Blue (`#2A468C`)  
> - **Accent (Strict 5%–10%):** Synergy Pink (`#AC1CA2`) — warm ambient rim light, gentle celebratory ribbon/bokeh, soft golden-pink hour glow.

---

## 1. Global Composition & Layout Guidelines

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                           TOP 55% - PRIME FOCAL ZONE                          |
|         (Keep faces, joyous expressions, embraces, bell, eye contact here)    |
|                                                                               |
|  LEFT FLANK (20%)   |      FLOATING TEXT/GRADIENT AREA     | RIGHT FLANK (20%)|
|  (Gentle background |   [H1: Headline & Subtitle Text]     | (Soft warm bokeh |
|   window light)     |    *DO NOT put faces in center base* |  or hospital art)|
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Aspect Ratio & Framing:**
   - **Canvas Ratio:** `16:9` or `21:9` (Ultra-wide landscape, rendered with `object-cover`).
   - **Vertical Safe Zone:** The top 50%–60% horizontal band is the primary focal zone. All human faces, embraces, celebratory bell gestures, and joyful expressions must be framed here.
   - **Bottom Occlusion:** The bottom 40% center contains the hero headline and subtitle over a gradient scrim. Keep the lower-center clean (soft table edge, lap, neutral background, or soft bokeh).
2. **Photography Style & Lighting:**
   - **Style:** Emotional, deeply authentic commercial healthcare portraiture and candid documentary photography. Shot on Hasselblad H6D-100c / 50mm f/1.4 lens.
   - **Lighting:** Warm, golden-hour morning daylight streaming through clean hospital bay windows, uplifting catchlights in the eyes, soft cinematic fill light.
   - **Authenticity:** Relatable, dignified Indian patients, survivors, and families spanning varied age groups. Real smiles of relief, triumph, and deep gratitude.
   - **No cliché tropes:** No bald cancer stereotypes, no sadness, no hospital gowns that look depressing. Focus on vitality, strength, life after cancer, and genuine family love.

### Section 1: What Does It Mean to Be a Cancer Survivor?
* **Slug:** `cancer-survivor-mean`
* **Route:** `/survivors/cancer-survivor-mean`
* **Theme Gradient:** `bg-linear-to-br from-rose-50 via-white to-orange-50`

#### A. Context from Written Copy
* **Hero Headline:** *"Stories of Strength, Hope, and Healing"*
* **Hero Subtitle:** *"Every cancer journey is different. Behind every diagnosis, there is a person, a family, and a story of courage."*
* **Core Context:** Survivorship starts from the moment of diagnosis and extends throughout life. It is not just about medical remission, but living with renewed purpose, physical vitality, and deep resilience.
* **Keywords:** Triumph, ringing the bell, resilience, milestone, renewed life, dignified strength, courage.

#### B. Aspect & Framing Guidelines
* **Composition:** Wide horizontal candid shot celebrating a treatment milestone (such as ringing the bronze Victory Bell in the hospital or standing proudly with healthy posture).
* **Safe Zone Rule:** The survivor’s uplifted face, smiling eyes, and the bell/raised hand must be positioned strictly in the upper 50% of the canvas.

#### C. Visual Message & Emotional Resonance
* **The Message:** Cancer does not define you; your courage and resilience do. Life after cancer is full of light, strength, and celebration.
* **Atmosphere:** Victorious, heartfelt, sun-drenched, triumphant, dignified.

#### D. Imagery Concept & Scene Description
* **Scene:** An Indian woman in her early 50s, radiant and healthy in an elegant pastel kurta, surrounded by a smiling oncologist and nursing staff clapping gently, as she joyfully rings the polished brass "Victory Bell" mounted on a modern wooden wall panel at Synergy Hospital.
* **Key Details:** Sunlight streaming through tall glass windows, soft focus on medical team clapping in Synergy Blue (`#2A468C`) scrubs, subtle celebratory pink accent ribbon or pink wall decor (`#AC1CA2`, 5%) in the background.
* **Color Palette:** Warm golden amber, soft rose, clean cream white, Synergy Blue (`#2A468C`), and 5%–8% subtle Synergy Pink (`#AC1CA2`) ambient warmth.

* **Slug:** `support-for-families`
* **Route:** `/survivors/support-for-families`
* **Theme Gradient:** `bg-linear-to-br from-teal-50 via-white to-emerald-50`

#### A. Context from Written Copy
* **Hero Headline:** *"Caring for the Caregivers"*
* **Hero Subtitle:** *"Cancer affects not only the patient, but the entire family. We are here to support you too."*
* **Core Context:** Caregivers carry an immense emotional and physical burden in silence. Synergy stands beside spouses, children, and parents with clear answers, emotional counseling, and practical guidance so the entire family heals together.
* **Keywords:** Family solidarity, caregiver empathy, holding hands, emotional relief, mutual strength, gentle counseling.

#### B. Aspect & Framing Guidelines
* **Composition:** Intimate mid-range horizontal portrait of a supportive family unit in a serene, sunlit hospital lounge or terrace garden.
* **Safe Zone Rule:** The emotional connection—intertwined hands, reassuring shoulder embrace, caring facial expressions—must reside across the upper 55% of the frame.

#### C. Visual Message & Emotional Resonance
* **The Message:** Caregivers are the unsung heroes of every healing journey. At Synergy, your family is wrapped in compassionate, expert support every single day.
* **Atmosphere:** Deeply touching, protective, serene, calming, affectionate.

#### D. Imagery Concept & Scene Description
* **Scene:** A poignant, uplifting moment between an adult Indian daughter and her recovering father seated comfortably on an upholstered bench in a sunlit hospital recovery lounge. The daughter gently holds her father’s hands with an expression of profound love and relief, while an empathetic family counselor stands supportively nearby.
* **Key Details:** Natural, genuine emotional intimacy without melodrama. Warm morning daylight, lush potted indoor plants, counselor wearing a subtle Synergy Blue (`#2A468C`) badge lanyard, gentle warm pink/magenta rim light (`#AC1CA2`, 5%) reflecting off the glass backdrop.
* **Color Palette:** Calming teal, warm terracotta, gentle beige, Synergy Blue (`#2A468C`), and 5% subtle Synergy Pink (`#AC1CA2`) ambient glow.
